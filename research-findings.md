### A. Ethereum Improvement Proposal (EIP-5725) Authorship
**Sources**:
- [eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-5725](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-5725)
- [github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/5725](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/5725)

**Title**: ERC-5725: Transferable Vesting NFT

**Role**: **Primary Author** (along with co-authors: Marco De Vries, Mario, DeFiFoFum, Elliott Green)

**Timeline**:
- **Created**: September 8, 2022
- **PR Initiated**: September 28, 2022
- **Successfully Merged**: November 25, 2022
- **Review Duration**: 2-month rigorous review process
- **Total Commits**: 27
- **Status**: DRAFT

**Technical Contribution**:
- Standardized interface for non-fungible tokens representing vesting schedules
- Addresses fragmentation in vesting contract implementations by providing unified framework
- Enables "transferable vesting NFTs which release underlying tokens over time"

**Key Features Defined**:
- Vesting Functions: `vestedPayout()`, `claimablePayout()`, `claimedPayout()`, `vestedPayoutAtTime()`
- Claim Management: Approval mechanisms separate from ERC-721 transfer rights
- Approved operators can claim vested tokens without transferring NFT
- Extends ERC-721, requires ERC-165 interface detection

**Use Cases**:
- Replicating SAFTs on-chain
- Secondary marketplaces for vesting assets
- Standardizing treasury instruments
- Aggregator platforms displaying multiple vesting schedules uniformly

**Review Process**:
- **Major Reviewers**: SamWilsn, lightclient, Pandapip1, xinbenlv
- **Supporting Contributors**: DeFiFoFum, JorgeAtPaladin
- Apegurus actively answered technical questions during review
- Confirmed support for vesting ERC20s from different contract instances with live use case
- Includes reference implementations, test suites, interface definitions

**Significance**: This is a major contribution to Ethereum's standard library, showing technical leadership at the protocol level and ability to navigate complex community review processes.
