# Design direction — founder portrait

The site should read as a considered introduction from a hands-on founder, not a
terminal dashboard or a consulting funnel. The composition is editorial: a calm
hero with the ape portrait as a recognizable signature, then a short progression
from current work to selected proof, leadership, personal context, and contact.

The existing off-black, warm off-white, restrained banana yellow, Space Grotesk,
and JetBrains Mono palette remains. Yellow is used for rules, labels, links, and
small moments of emphasis only. Large typography is reserved for the founder
statement and section titles; body copy is sized for comfortable reading.

Selected work uses varied editorial panels rather than a repeated card grid:
ApeSwap leads with protocol metrics, ERC-5725/ApeBond carries the technical
detail and its own clearly attributed protocol totals, and Lynex forms a concise
third proof point with sourced protocol metrics. Endure is the visual and
editorial priority in the current-focus section. The mobile layout stacks into
clear, generously padded reading blocks with no sticky contact control.
